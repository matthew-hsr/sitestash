FAQ

How do I save a page?

Tap + in the app and paste a URL, or use the Share sheet in Safari or Chrome and choose Save to Sitestash.

What does "Entire site" do?

It follows links on the page to save a whole site for offline reading. It's a Sitestash Pro feature — unlock it once, or watch a rewarded ad for a few free saves.

Do saved pages work with no internet?

Yes. Pages are stored as self-contained files (images and styles included), so they render fully offline.

Where is my data?

Everything stays on your device. There's no account and no server. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I search inside saved pages?

Use the search bar in the library — it matches titles, URLs, tags, and the full body text of saved pages.
