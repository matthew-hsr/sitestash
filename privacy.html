Sitestash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Sitestash ("the app") is published by Sitestash ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

We do not run a server and there is no account. The pages you save, the library index, your tags, and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive, sell, or share that content. On-device analysis (language, keywords, reading time, full-text search) is performed locally using Apple's frameworks and never leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, Google's SDK may collect and process device and advertising identifiers (including the IDFA, with your permission) and coarse usage data to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and its advertising terms. Where required, we present a consent choice before requesting personalized ads.

Your choices

iOS asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the app uses the advertising identifier for tracking. You can decline, and change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Declining does not remove ads, but limits personalization.

Purchases

"Sitestash Pro" is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Network requests

When you save a page, the app fetches that URL (and its assets) directly from the site you chose, so it can be read offline. We do not proxy or log those requests on a server.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
